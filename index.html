THE IDEA 01 / 03

Look at two
words at a time.

A bigram is a pair of neighboring words. In “the cat sat on the mat,” the first pair is “the cat,” followed by “cat sat,” and so on.

WHAT THE MODEL SEES 03 / 03

Word pairs

Every neighboring pair, in order.

2

Next-word table

What each word was followed by.

3

Next-word guess

The most common neighbor wins.

When two next words occur equally often, the model picks the one it saw first. It only looks one word back, so it doesn’t understand meaning.
